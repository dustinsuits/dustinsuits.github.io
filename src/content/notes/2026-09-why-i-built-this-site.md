title: "Why I built this site"
date: 2026-09-23
summary: "A place for both halves of what I do — security and IT operations, and the woodworking on the side."
---
I wanted a place online that actually reflects what I do, not just a job history. I lead security and IT operations by day, and I run a small CNC/laser woodworking business, Shop Humble, on the side — most bios only ever show one of those.

So this site holds both: an [About](/about/) page in my actual voice, a condensed [Experience](/experience/) page for anyone who wants the resume view, a [Projects](/projects/) section split between Shop Humble builds and security/IT side projects, and this [Notes](/notes/) section for shorter, less formal updates.

It's built with Astro and deployed automatically to GitHub Pages on every push, so adding new content going forward is as easy as writing a markdown file and pushing. First real project write-ups and photos are coming soon.
