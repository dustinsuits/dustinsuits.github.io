Hi, I'm Dustin. On paper I'm a security and IT operations guy — these days leading that side of the house at [TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com). Before that, I did the hands-on version of the same job: global networks, Tier 3/4 support, and a lot of macOS and iOS fleets.

I like the unglamorous work: tightening security posture, writing the runbook that should have existed already, and building systems that hold up when something actually breaks.

Off the clock, I run [Shop Humble](/projects/), a small woodworking business where I design and build custom home décor and wood pieces. The process runs from rough lumber to a finished, CNC-cut and laser-engraved piece someone puts in their home. It started as a way to make things with my hands after a career spent making things run smoothly on a screen, and it's turned into a real second craft.

Those two worlds — server racks and sawdust — feel less like a contradiction and more like the same instinct pointed in two directions: take something ambiguous or chaotic, and turn it into something documented, repeatable, and built to last. Somewhere further back there's also a few years running live audio for basketball games, which explains why I still can't sit through a bad PA mix without wincing.

This site is where both halves live — the professional background on [Experience](/experience/), the actual builds on [Projects](/projects/), and whatever I'm currently into on [Now](/now/).
